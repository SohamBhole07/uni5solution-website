ABOUT US

We got tired of Wi-Fi
projects that stop at install.

uni5solution is an enterprise Wi-Fi and network infrastructure company built by field engineers who were tired of watching "completed" networks fail within a month. We survey first, design second, and stay on after handover.

OUR STORY

Started in Mumbai.
Built for how buildings actually work.

uni5solution was founded by a small team of wireless and structured-cabling engineers who kept getting called in to fix networks that looked fine on paper — access points mounted in the wrong spot, no capacity planning for peak occupancy, guest and corporate traffic sharing the same VLAN.

We built the company around one rule: no access point goes up before a real site survey confirms it belongs there. That discipline is why offices, hotels and campuses across Mumbai and Pune now hand us their networks and don't think about them again.
