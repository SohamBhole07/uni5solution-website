uni5solution has spent over a decade building the infrastructure businesses in Mumbai and Pune run on — from the internet line coming into the building to the last CCTV camera and power socket.

OUR STORY

Founded on a simple idea:
one accountable partner.

Most businesses end up juggling separate vendors for their internet line, their Wi-Fi, their CCTV, their firewall and their electrical work — and when something breaks, nobody quite owns the problem. uni5solution was built to be the one number you call instead.

Under founder Sachin Patil, the company has grown from internet lease line services into a full IT, networking, security and electrical solutions provider, delivering supply, installation and comprehensive AMC support across Mumbai and Pune.
