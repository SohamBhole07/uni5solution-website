INDUSTRIES

Different buildings.
Different failure points.

A warehouse and a boutique hotel need entirely different Wi-Fi designs. Here's how we approach the sectors we work in most.

CORPORATE

Offices & Business Parks

High device density, video conferencing traffic and hot-desking mean roaming has to be seamless across every floor.

HOSPITALITY

Hotels & Resorts

Guest-facing bandwidth, branded portals and coverage that reaches every room — including the ones with thick concrete walls.

RETAIL

Malls & Retail Chains

POS reliability, footfall analytics and guest Wi-Fi that doesn't compete with in-store payment terminals for bandwidth.

HEALTHCARE

Hospitals & Clinics

Mission-critical uptime for connected medical devices, with strict segmentation between clinical and patient guest networks.

EDUCATION

Schools & Campuses

Coverage across classrooms, hostels and outdoor areas, sized for a BYOD population that peaks at class-change hours.

CO-WORKING

Shared Workspaces

Per-tenant network isolation and usage reporting, on infrastructure that can be reconfigured as membership changes.

INDUSTRIAL

Warehousing & Manufacturing

Outdoor-rated access points, high-ceiling coverage and connectivity for handheld scanners and IoT sensors on the floor.

EVENTS

Banquet Halls & Venues

Temporary high-density deployments for conferences and weddings, torn down and redeployed without residual downtime.

A CLOSER LOOK

Two sectors, two very different briefs.

HOSPITALITY

What we optimise for

Guest satisfaction scores tied directly to Wi-Fi complaints. We plan for streaming-level bandwidth per room, branded splash pages that match your property's identity, and coverage in stairwells and basements that generic installs skip.

CORPORATE / IT

What we optimise for

Zero-touch roaming for hybrid teams moving between meeting rooms and desks, VLAN separation for BYOD, and reporting your IT team can pull without calling us for every export.
