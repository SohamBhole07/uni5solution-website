01 — CORE SERVICE

Enterprise Wi-Fi Design & Deployment

We design your wireless network around your building, not a generic access point count. That means RF modelling, capacity planning for peak device density, and access point placement that accounts for walls, floor material and interference sources before anything is mounted.

  • — Predictive and on-site RF site surveys
  • — Access point selection, placement and channel planning
  • — Capacity planning for peak occupancy and device density
  • — Roaming and seamless handoff configuration for large floor plates

02 — ONGOING SUPPORT

Managed Wi-Fi & NOC Support

Once a network is live, our Network Operations Centre keeps watching it. We monitor uptime, channel interference and access point health, and step in before your team files a ticket. Firmware updates and configuration changes are scheduled outside business hours by default.

03 — HOSPITALITY & RETAIL

Guest & Branded Wi-Fi Portals

For hotels, malls and F&B venues, guest Wi-Fi is a brand touchpoint as much as a utility. We build branded captive portals with social or OTP login, bandwidth shaping per guest, and usage analytics — kept on a separate VLAN from your operational network.

  • — Branded splash pages and social/OTP login
  • — Per-guest bandwidth limits and time-based access
  • — Footfall and dwell-time analytics dashboard
TYPICAL USE CASE

A 180-room hotel

Lobby, banquet and floor-by-floor guest Wi-Fi on a branded portal, isolated from POS and back-office systems, with bandwidth reserved for streaming during peak check-in hours.

WHY IT MATTERS

Wireless is only as good as the wire behind it

Under-specced cabling is the most common cause of a Wi-Fi network that looks right on paper but underperforms in practice. We size cable runs and switch capacity for tomorrow's device count, not today's.

04 — WIRED FOUNDATION

Structured Cabling & LAN

Cat6 and Cat6A structured cabling, patch panels, server racks and PoE switching — installed and certified to back a wireless network that has to carry real load, not just a demo.

05 — DIAGNOSTIC

Wi-Fi Site Survey & Heatmapping

Before we recommend anything, we measure. Our surveys map signal strength, channel interference and dead zones floor by floor, producing a heatmap you can review before committing to a design.

  • — Predictive survey from architectural floor plans
  • — On-site validation with calibrated survey hardware
  • — Interference analysis across 2.4GHz, 5GHz and 6GHz bands
STANDARD ON EVERY BUILD

Segmented by default

Corporate, guest and IoT traffic sit on separate VLANs from day one — not as an add-on after a security review flags it.

06 — SECURITY

Network Security & Access Control

WPA3 encryption, RADIUS-based authentication, VLAN segmentation and firewall policy that keeps a compromised guest device from ever touching your corporate network.
