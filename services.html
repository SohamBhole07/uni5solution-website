PILLAR 01 — IT & NETWORKING CONNECTIVITY

The connection your business runs on.

Internet Lease Line Services

A lease line is a dedicated, symmetric telecommunications circuit connecting two locations — sometimes called a "private circuit." Unlike a broadband or PSTN connection, it's exclusively yours: always-on, monitored round the clock, with equal upload and download speeds. That makes it the right choice for VPN access, ERP systems, client-server applications and hosted web applications, all backed by a pre-known, budgetable static cost.

We provision lease line and point-to-point connectivity through Class A ISPs and telecom partners — Airtel, Sify, Reliance, Vodafone, MTNL, BSNL, Tata Communications and VSNL — at any location in India, with a dual-gateway design so your link stays up even if one carrier's backbone goes down.

WHY A LEASE LINE OVER BROADBAND
  • — Always-on, dedicated connection — not shared with other users
  • — Symmetric speeds: uploads as fast as downloads
  • — Static IP, essential for VPN and hosted applications
  • — Delivered on copper, fibre optic or wireless transmission
  • — Monitored round the clock with defined SLAs
  • — Suitable for web hosting and serving hundreds of users at once
